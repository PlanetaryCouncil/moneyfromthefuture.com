  **Palestine** divides its panoramic field between catastrophe and an image of peace. On the left, soldiers and a bulldozer move through shattered buildings and fallen bodies beneath a sky fractured by red lightning; on the right, the Dome of the Rock stands in clear blue light.

  A violent red-black surge occupies the boundary between the two worlds, gradually dissolving into delicate golden geometry. Above the open plaza, a luminous white dove carries an olive branch, echoing the smaller dove medallions set into the ornate floral border.

  The stark split refuses to separate present destruction from the hope of a different future. Sacred architecture, military machinery and the universal peace emblem are placed in direct confrontation, making the work both a memorial image and an appeal for violence to give way to dignity and coexistence.
