  **Krakow** presents Wawel Castle glowing above the Vistula at sunset, its towers and red roofs reflected in the dark water below. Pink, red and blue circuitry overlays the sky, transforming the historic cityscape into the luminous center of a futuristic forty-two-złoty note.

  On the right, a large dragon assembled from bright network lines rises beside the castle, recalling Kraków’s enduring Wawel Dragon legend. Polish eagles, city seals, repeated denominations and the phrases “Bank of Vibes” and “Valid for One Good Conversation” give civic pride a playful unofficial currency.

  The work connects history and local mythology with contemporary social energy. Rather than freezing Kraków as a museum city, it treats architecture, stories and conversation as living forms of value that can be carried forward into a networked future.
