The idea

The Title Is The Medium.

It could be a meme.
It could be for real.

The genre is mindfuck and performative meme realism. The engine is hyperstition, not superstition: a fiction that makes itself real by being believed and acted on. Self-fulfilling prophecies. Memes that turn into facts.

The sentence on the label does the work. Read once, it sounds like an offer. Read again, it is a hypothetical: could. Then you see the price is in bitcoin, and you start to wonder whether anyone will actually pay it. Whatever you decide, you've already joined in. Buyers don't just own this piece. They become part of it.

This is the second experiment on this site. The first is a picture that contains the number describing itself. This one contains the conversation that decided whether it was allowed to exist.

Look at the bottom edge

The Clearance Is Glued Into The Piece.

Before the title was final, the artist asked an AI model whether a title like this could work. The printed screenshots of that conversation are stuck along the bottom of the canvas, as part of the composition. The question, the answer and the reasoning, "I could… = hypothetical, conditional, artistic", sit next to a 0-pound Money From The Future note, a Polish 50 złoty note, and a hologram sticker that says ORIGINAL, tied on with jute string.

03 · The terms

Terms And Conditions, With A Commit History.

Most artworks come with a certificate. This one comes with a public Git repository. It is a working draft that changes in the open, and its commit log is part of the piece. What "1 night" actually means is written there, not in the title.

README.mdThe working draft. "I remain in full control" is the essential term.
ARTIST-CONTROL.mdA purchase gives nobody authority over anyone's body. Either side can decline, pause or stop.
CONSENT.mdConsent must be specific, revocable and given fresh. A title, a payment or silence never counts.
SERVICE.mdThe proposed minimum: 24 one-hour Ultra Magic Session massages, or live presence over Zoom.
REGULATIONS.md"We will decide later": coin-flip regulation, humidity measurement, trial by combat, the Mecha-Godzilla Clause.
TRIBUNAL.mdFive layers of appeal, every office held by the artist. Satire, disclosed as such.
CHOICE-OF-LAW.mdGoverning law: not chosen yet.
MINIMAL-DISCLAIMER.md"Precise ambiguity. Feature, not a bug."
What it is

An interactive, performative artwork. The title sets a mood, and the terms set the deliverable: massage sessions or live presence, scoped before anything happens. The paid deliverable includes no sexual activity.

Why it's on GitHub

Contracts are usually signed once and then frozen. This one is versioned, diffable and forkable, like code, and every amendment carries a timestamp. Couldism, wouldism and shouldism prevail, and the negotiation is part of the artwork.

Look closer

It Rewards A Second Look.

The piece is built like a pinboard of one life at one moment. It holds official paperwork and personal photographs, money from three countries and none, and slogans that argue with each other. It also lights up.
